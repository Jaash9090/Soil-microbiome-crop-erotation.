Microbiome Diversity Analyzer

A computational analysis tool for microbial abundance tables. It calculates standard ecological diversity indices from the data you provide. It does not identify microbes from raw DNA, train an AI model, or scientifically validate crop recommendations.

1. Enter microbial abundance data

Paste a two-column CSV table (taxon,abundance) or upload a CSV file. Abundance may be sequencing read counts or relative abundance values. Use your own measured data for actual sample analysis.

These metrics describe only the submitted abundance table. They do not establish soil health, crop suitability, microbial function, or agricultural model accuracy. For meaningful conclusions, compare appropriate replicated samples and consult domain experts.

3. Methodology & limitations

For each taxon, relative abundance pᵢ = abundanceᵢ / total abundance. Shannon diversity H′ = −Σpᵢ ln(pᵢ). Gini–Simpson diversity = 1 − Σpᵢ². Pielou evenness = H′ / ln(S), for S > 1. Zero-count taxa are excluded from observed richness.
